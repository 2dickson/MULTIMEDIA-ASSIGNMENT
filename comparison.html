What to notice

Listen for changes in clarity, artifacts, and background noise. Simple tones may not reveal meaningful differences; real-world recordings are more useful for critical listening.

Summary

Lossless formats preserve decoded samples; lossy formats trade some information for smaller files. Sample rate, bit depth, bitrate, encoder settings, and playback equipment influence perceived quality.
