UNDERSTANDING COMPRESSION

Lossless vs Lossy Audio

UNCOMPRESSED

Uncompressed Audio

Formats such as WAV PCM and AIFF PCM store audio samples without perceptual compression. They generally require more storage space.

LOSSLESS

Lossless Compression

Formats such as FLAC reduce file size without permanently discarding audio information. The original audio samples can be reconstructed.

LOSSY

Lossy Compression

Formats such as MP3, AAC, OGG Vorbis and Opus reduce file size by removing selected audio information. Quality depends on bitrate and encoding settings.
